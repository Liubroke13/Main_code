In this study, we present a theoretical investigation of excitonic transitions in two dimensional
materials under photoexcitation of scalar and vector vortex beams.

Adopting the Coulomb gauge is justified bythe theory of light--matter interaction and resulting the longitudinal component which shows spin-orbital interaction (SOI) in both its amplitude and phase.
